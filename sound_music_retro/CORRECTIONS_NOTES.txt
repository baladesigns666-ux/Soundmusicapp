SOUND MUSIC - CORRECTIONS

1) NOW PLAYING
- Double-tap the LEFT half of the vinyl to seek backward 10 seconds.
- Double-tap the RIGHT half of the vinyl to seek forward 10 seconds.
- The heart/favourite action remains on the separate heart button.

2) CROSSFADE
- Uses equal-power sine/cosine volume curves across the two audio decks.
- Incoming track source now carries its MediaItem metadata.
- Failed incoming-track setup restores the outgoing deck instead of stopping it.
- Cancelling an active crossfade invalidates pending fade steps and restores the foreground volume.
- Manual next, queue edits, and clear operations cancel an active fade before changing playback state.

3) PLAY QUEUE
- Current track and upcoming tracks are displayed in separate sections.
- Upcoming tracks can be reordered with the drag handle.
- Upcoming tracks can be removed individually.
- CLEAR NEXT removes upcoming tracks but keeps the current track playing.
